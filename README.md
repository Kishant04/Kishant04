## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Software Engineering student at **Universiti Malaya** with an interest in software development, mobile application development, and building practical solutions to real-world problems.

Through my academic projects, hackathons, and internship experience, I have gained exposure to different technologies and the software development process. I enjoy learning new tools, collaborating with others, and improving my programming and problem-solving skills.

## 📚 Currently Learning: WIF3005 Software Maintenance and Evolution

I’ve worked on various assignments, projects, and hackathons, though most of them were one-off developments. We code, we push, we present, Done. However, in real industry, codebases are maintained, improved and undergo multiple versions.

Through **WIF3005 Software Maintenance and Evolution**, I hope to understand how software systems are maintained, improved, and adapted to changing requirements over time.

I want to learn more about identifying and fixing bugs, refactoring code, understanding existing codebases, and applying software evolution techniques.

I also expect this course to help me appreciate the importance of writing maintainable code, managing technical debt, and ensuring software remains reliable and relevant in the long run.
